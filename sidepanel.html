See where your mail actually lives.

A Google sign-in window will open so you can choose an account. MailBoy reads your mailbox, and adds or removes folders when you ask it to — it can never delete a message for good. It talks to Gmail directly from this browser, so your mail is never sent to a server of ours.

Log out of MailBoy?

What MailBoy has measured for this mailbox stays on this device for 7 days, so signing back in is immediate. After that it is erased, and the next sign-in do a full scan — which can take many minutes on a large mailbox.
